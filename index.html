Trust architecture

Powerful does not mean uncontrolled.

SakthiAI is being built with provider neutrality, explicit data boundaries, exportability, cost policy and safe failure as core architecture — not release-day patches.

01Free-first routing

Paid providers remain disabled unless the owner deliberately approves them.

02Truthful capability states

No button may imply a backend exists when the runtime is unavailable.

03Portable by design

Projects, evidence and artifacts should remain exportable and replaceable.

04Approval boundaries

Publishing, messaging, deployments and destructive actions require policy.
